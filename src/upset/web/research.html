How to read this. Every number counts only UFC fights strictly before the chosen date, within the chosen window. Rates use summed counts over summed fight time. Knockdowns, takedowns and submission attempts are expressed per 15 minutes of actual fight time, including fights that end early; this is a standardized rate, not an average per fight; control figures use bouts where both fighters' control time was recorded. Percentiles compare fighters with at least 3 timed fights and 15 minutes in the same window and cutoff. Division means the latest classified UFC bout before the cutoff. Peer pools use that assignment, while Career and last-N statistics can include fights in earlier divisions. The pool uses the same division when at least 25 fighters qualify, otherwise all divisions; its scope is shown beside the ranking. They are descriptive, not adjusted for opponent quality, and not predictions. Date filters use currently reviewed results and do not reconstruct when an amendment became known. Height, reach and profile weight come from the stored profile snapshot, not a live weigh-in or roster feed.
