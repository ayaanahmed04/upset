How to read this. Every number counts only UFC fights strictly before the chosen date, within the chosen window. Rates use summed counts over summed fight time. Knockdowns, takedowns and submission attempts are shown per 5 minutes of actual fight time; control figures use bouts where both fighters' control time was recorded. Percentiles compare fighters with at least 3 timed fights and 15 minutes in the same window and cutoff. The pool uses the same division when at least 25 fighters qualify, otherwise all divisions; its scope is shown beside the ranking. They are descriptive, not adjusted for opponent quality, and not predictions. Date filters use currently reviewed results and do not reconstruct when an amendment became known. Height, reach and weight come from the profile snapshot.
