**The significant-strike accuracy validation milestone is complete.** The next
research step is a separate study of exposure-dependent dispersion and
chronological mean drift/calibration. No adjusted statistic is ready for public
release, and the website continues to show descriptive raw statistics.

The new validation reproduced all 17,708 original predictions, independently
reconstructed their histories, and retained the exact 7,028 matched development
sides and 5,279 tuning sides. The mean strength remains k=500; no post-result
retuning or October 31 outcomes were used.

Bout variation improves conditional count prediction and meets the declared
overall PIT bands. It still fails 11 of 40 overlapping subgroup labels, including
most actual-attempt bins and several duration groups; one division group is
too thin. The mean underpredicts attempt-weighted accuracy by 1.78 percentage
points. Split-half correlations are descriptive and depend strongly on which
career-survival and exposure criteria select the sample. A minimum-history
release threshold has not been established.

The decision is to retain the fixed mean and bout-variation model as offline
research references, reject stationary-history uncertainty as adequate for
the observed count variation, and keep raw values on the site. Opponent
adjustment, protected future evidence, validated latent-skill intervals and
versioned serving snapshots/windows remain unfinished requirements.
