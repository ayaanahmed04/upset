# Provider correspondence and data-use scope

An October 4, 2026 support reply explicitly permits using the supplied
information on a privately operated site, building tools, redistribution and
integration into apps, and states that the provider imposes no restrictions
from its side. Preserve the original request and response as project evidence.
Earlier correspondence also permits retaining collected responses for personal
noncommercial analysis after subscription cancellation.

This written reply is broader than the September 2026 published terms, which
permit attributed application display but restrict data-feed/API redistribution
without a separate written agreement. Record both sources rather than treating
the purchase price as a blanket licence. The provider does not grant third-party
rights to photographs, logos, video or other media. The correspondence supports
proceeding with the project's statistical application; it is not proof that
the provider is an official league feed or that a dataset is exclusive.

Published source: https://citoapi.com/terms/
