# UPSET overall rating v4: smoother upper grades

This display-only candidate is declared before evaluating it. It addresses the size of
badge moves around the jump from the high 80s to the low 90s. It is not a new
fight prediction model or an era adjustment.

Keep the verified v3 database, cutoff, historical reference, 90 and 99
thresholds, below-90 grading rule, and all unrounded model scores unchanged.
Retain the 50/50 résumé and skills blend, provisional and limited-history rules,
five-point limit, and result-direction holds. Earlier displayed changes can
affect subsequent badges through those same protections.

Place grades 91–98 between the fixed 90 and 99 thresholds so that the nine
gaps grow in equal steps, from one-half to one-and-a-half times their average
width. The reference remains fixed and no fighter is assigned an individual grade.

Compare with the verified v3 release on identical fighter dates and decisive bouts.
Before considering the candidate for local use, require:

- Exact reproduction of v3 ratings and pre-fight badge evidence. Retain all
  non-display fields exactly; rerun the truncated history ending before
  2024-01-01 and require the same v4 rows through that date.
- No wrong-direction decisive updates. On the shared set of at least 500 bouts
  where both versions can distinguish the fighters, the higher badge's historical
  win rate must not fall by more than two percentage points.
- Among fighters with a linked UFC bout since 2025-01-01, 1%–5% at 95+,
  no more at 90+ than v3, and no more than half the v2 count at 98.
  At most ten distinct fighters may have reached 99 in the linked archive.
- For the fixed cohort of decisive fighter updates from 2019-01-01 with a
  prior v3 model grade of 89–95 and an absolute raw combined change of at most
  1.25 points, use at least 50 matched updates and require a reduction of
  at least 20% in the frequency of displayed moves of four or more points.
  A decisive update requires a prior state and the same win or loss outcome
  across all of that fighter's bouts on that date.

Also report full-archive cohort sizes, all post-2018 decisive moves of exactly
five points and actual five-point limit applications, current and peak example
ratings, and the fixed grade thresholds. Examples illustrate the outcome;
they are not acceptance rules. This comparison is retrospective development
evidence. Use v3 if the candidate fails, and preserve v3 if the candidate passes.
