# UPSET Experimental v3 grading protocol

Declared November 5, 2026 before running the candidate on the saved histories.
This is a local experimental display recalibration. Start with the verified v2
snapshot for the same reviewed database and retain its underlying components,
combined scores, probability assessment, dated states, and recorded outcomes.

Keep the historical v2 grade below the 90 boundary. Define the boundary as
the linear 95.2nd percentile of the same pre-2019 combined scores, the nominal
v2 half-up rounding boundary for 90. Below it the grade cannot exceed 89.
For the 99 threshold, take the mean of the three highest distinct fighters'
pre-2019 career peaks, each based on states with at least five recorded bouts.
Each qualifying fighter contributes at most one peak. This gives the benchmark
several exceptional careers and limits the influence of any single outlier.
Divide the interval from 90 to the 99 threshold into nine equal bands of
combined-score units for 90 through 98. The threshold is inclusive for 99.
A fighter's latest assessed combined score determines their model grade.
The existing limited-history cap, five-point movement limit, outcome holds,
and historical peak calculation still apply in the same order as v2.

Compare v2 and v3 on the same verified development bouts through October 25,
2026, using strictly earlier grades. Record ties, unavailable bouts, and
accuracy on the common unequal sample. Reproduce the v2 track record first.
Check all histories, including prefix replay through 2023, unchanged model
inputs, and correct caps, holds, and newly earned or held 99s. Inspect the
latest grade per fighter with a recorded bout since January 1, 2025. Local
activation requires at least 500 common unequal fights, accuracy within two
percentage points of v2 or better, zero wrong-direction decisive updates,
no more than half as many 98 badges in that recent group, no increase in the
90+ count there, a 95+ share between 1% and 5% in that group, and at most ten
distinct fighters reaching 99 across the full archived history. Include named
examples for interpretation. Individual fighter grades do not decide the gate.

Run one separate research comparison for era drift. At each update, center
Elo around the mean Elo from the latest strictly earlier state per fighter in
the preceding 730 days. Use 1500 as the mean when fewer than 30 prior peers
are available. Apply the same résumé transform and blend. Use the centered
values as latched on each fighter's own last update. Compare each variant's
post-2018 share above its own pre-2019 90 boundary and report yearly results.
Fit a positive probability slope on 2012–2018 and score both on the identical
v2 development fights. This diagnostic stays separate from the v3 serving
decision. The historical development sample has already been inspected.

In the viewer label the last recorded UFC date and indicate when more than
two years separate the assessed bout and the selected page cutoff. Preserve
the compact octagon. When a display adjustment applies, explain the model
grade and the displayed grade together in the breakdown.
